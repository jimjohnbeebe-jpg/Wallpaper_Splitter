# Privacy policy

**Wallpaper Splitter**, published by Maybe LLC ([maybellc.com](https://maybellc.com)).
Effective 6 November 2026.

## Summary

Wallpaper Splitter does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry, and it makes no network requests.

## What the app does with your files

Everything happens on your own PC:

- **Images you open** are read from your disk to show the preview and make the wallpapers. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Wallpaper files** the app exports are saved in `Pictures\Wallpaper Splitter`. Windows reads your wallpaper from them.
- **Settings** (your last image, framing, monitor sizes and preferences) are saved in `%APPDATA%\WallpaperSplitter\settings.json`.
- **A preview cache** is kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WallpaperSplitter\cache`, and the app's built-in web view keeps its own data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.jimbeebe.wallpapersplitter`.
- **Windows settings:** when you ask it to, the app sets each monitor's wallpaper and can turn a monitor's HDR on or off.

The Microsoft Store version keeps the same files inside its own package folder, and Windows removes them when you uninstall the app. For the portable version, delete the exe and the folders above.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Maybe LLC receives no personal information about you from the Store.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
